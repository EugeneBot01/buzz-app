# Channel shortcuts

A row of links at the top of a channel that everyone in it sees.

## Getting started

1. In a channel, choose the link button at the top.
2. For a Google Meet room, choose the link at the bottom to make one, then paste its address. Paste an address (https only) and an optional name, then choose **Add**.
3. Choose **Save**. Everyone in the channel sees the new links.
